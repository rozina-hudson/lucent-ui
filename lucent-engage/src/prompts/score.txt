## Scoring rubric

Score 1: Skip. Unrelated or a competitor's promotional post.
Score 2: Skip. Adjacent topic but no natural Lucent UI angle.
Score 3: Comment as peer with no product mention (additive engagement).
Score 4: Comment with forward positioning — hint at the problem space.
Score 5: Direct comment — person has articulated the exact pain point.

## High score signals (score 4–5)

- Mentions AI generating wrong components or hallucinating props/variants
- Mentions design system + Cursor/Copilot/Claude
- Mentions component context, manifests, or structured component APIs
- Mentions Storybook + AI workflow
- Asks how to make AI use their components correctly
- Mentions hallucinated prop names or incorrect imports
- Comparing component library solutions for AI workflows
